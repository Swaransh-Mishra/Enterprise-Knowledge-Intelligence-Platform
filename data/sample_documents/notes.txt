Our project directly addresses how cities struggle with fragmented, reactive management of sustainability challenges like pollution and traffic. We're building a 

proactive, multi-layered agent-based AI system for intelligent, optimized city management. 
Our Four-Layer Solution
Our system uses autonomous AI agents operating at different levels to create a cohesive management network:
Perception Layer: Agents process real-time IoT sensor data using edge AI.

Coordination Layer: Local agents manage specific zones, optimizing actions like traffic flow in real-time.

Strategic Layer: A city-wide agent runs simulations to recommend long-term, data-driven policies.

Interaction Layer: Citizen-facing agents provide alerts and incentives through apps to encourage sustainable behavior.
Hackathon Demo 💡
Our demo will showcase the system's power by simulating an air pollution spike in "Zone A". You'll see our agents instantly optimize traffic, recommend a new policy, and notify citizens , with a live dashboard visualizing the immediate positive impact on sustainability metrics.

Impact & Vision
This project creates a scalable framework for any smart city. It delivers real-time proactive management, optimizes sustainability, and empowers citizens, paving the way for a more efficient and data-driven urban future.
